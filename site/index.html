About Us

Welcome to aashirwad

Located around Bansilal Chowk in Hazaribagh, aashirwad offers wholesome, authentic North Indian, Tandoori, Mughlai, and Indo-Chinese food. We take pride in fresh preparations, clay-oven tandoori breads, rich gravies, steamed momos, and aromatic biryanis. All orders placed on our website enjoy complimentary home delivery across Hazaribagh.
